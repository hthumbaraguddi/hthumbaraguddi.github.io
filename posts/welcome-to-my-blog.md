Hello world! Welcome to the very first post on my new platform.

This space is dedicated to chronicling my journey through software development, architecture, and technology exploration. Instead of relying on a heavyweight JS framework or CMS, I decided to build this setup relying purely on modern, vanilla HTML, CSS, and JS. And sometimes, **simplicity is the ultimate sophistication**.

## Why Vanilla?
In an ecosystem bombarded with Next.js, Nuxt, Astro, and SvelteKit, building a static site entirely from scratch feels slightly rebellious. But the core web technologies have evolved so well that we can attain elegant, highly-performant aesthetics rapidly without thousands of dependencies.

> "The complexity of our dependencies is directly proportional to our anxiety during deployments."
